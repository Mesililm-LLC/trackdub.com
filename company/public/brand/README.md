# Mesalim logo assets

The wordmark combines the custom M from the reviewed concept 03 with outlined Instrument Serif lettering and an amber period. The M retains the reference's sweeping diagonal and open inner detail; it is not the font's stock M. These SVGs have no runtime font dependency.

The full burgundy wordmark is used in the shared company header and footer. The standalone custom M supplies the favicon and touch icon. The logo remains branded Mesalim; it does not introduce a legal entity suffix.

Artwork was prepared in the Mesalim design workspace from the user-reviewed custom-wordmark package. The source font is Instrument Serif Regular, with its SIL Open Font License preserved under `/licenses/Instrument-Serif-OFL.txt`. Ink, burgundy, amber, and cream follow the company site's existing design tokens.
